# Publication Timeline — Geopolitical Risk and the Real Economy

**Event:** Feb 28, 2026 — Strait of Hormuz / Iran Crisis
**Paper:** Industry-level transmission of geopolitical risk to the US real economy

## Why We Cannot Publish Now (March 2026)

As of early March 2026 (~4 days post-event), we have almost no usable post-event data:

- **Event study:** Only 1 post-event trading day — far too few for meaningful CARs or statistical significance
- **VAR model:** Pre-event only — no post-event monthly macro data exists yet
- **BLS Employment, CPI, PPI:** Latest data is January 2026 (pre-event)
- **Industrial Production:** January 2026 (pre-event)
- **GDP by Industry (BEA):** Q4 2025 at best — Q1 2026 won't arrive until late April

Bottom line: we can describe the shock but cannot measure its transmission to the real economy.
