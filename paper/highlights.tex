\noindent\textbf{Highlights}

\bigskip

\begin{itemize}
\item Pre-event oil beta strongly predicts cross-sectional sectoral CARs across five geopolitical oil shocks ($\gamma_1 = +20.18$, $p < 0.001$)
\item A bounded Aggregation Masking Index ($\Psi$) reveals crisis-period masking exceeds non-event masking by 9.5 percentage points
\item The oil-beta transmission channel operates asymmetrically: positively for supply-side shocks and negatively for demand-side shocks
\item Dynamic analysis shows the oil-beta effect builds persistently from $t+1$ to $t+15$, indicating sectoral reallocation rather than short-term overreaction
\item Event-specific beta estimation across all five events confirms the cross-sectional pattern is stable over time
\end{itemize}
