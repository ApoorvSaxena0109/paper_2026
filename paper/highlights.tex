\noindent\textbf{Highlights}

\bigskip

\begin{itemize}
\item Oil price sensitivity predicts sectoral equity responses to geopolitical shocks across five major events
\item A bounded Aggregation Masking Index shows that crisis-period masking significantly exceeds non-event levels
\item The oil-beta channel operates asymmetrically: positively for supply shocks and negatively for demand shocks
\item The sectoral reallocation effect builds persistently over time rather than reversing
\item Cross-country evidence links energy import dependence to international heterogeneity in shock responses
\end{itemize}
